# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends using SQL, MySQL, Python, and Power BI.

The dashboard aims to help HR managers identify the key factors influencing employee attrition, understand workforce demographics, evaluate employee satisfaction, and support data-driven retention strategies.

## Business Goals

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Identify high-risk departments and job roles.
- Understand demographic trends.
- Evaluate salary and overtime impact.
- Monitor workforce KPIs.
- Support HR decision-making through interactive dashboards.
